Research interests

  • Property-preserving numerical methods. Optimization-based cell-average limiters and discontinuous Galerkin methods for two-phase flow on non-matching meshes; metric projection formulations and convergence analysis for convection-diffusion equations.
  • Porous media flow and poromechanics. Finite element methods for Biot's poroelasticity and two-phase flow in heterogeneous porous media.
  • Data assimilation. Continuous data assimilation for Biot's system and ensemble score filtering for state estimation in two-phase flow models with rare earth materials.
